## [A] Resolution Center 회신 (영어)

Hello App Review team,

Thank you for the review of Fatesaid 1.2.0. We understand the concern about the number of astrology and fortune apps, and we agree that our previous Home screen leaned that way: its first card was a daily card, followed by a chart. We have changed the app, not just its wording, and we are submitting a new build with this reply.

**What Fatesaid is.** A self-reflection tool built around one path: take a self-insight test, talk it through in a guided conversation, and get a personal report built from your own answers. It uses the Four Pillars (Saju) framework as a structured way to describe temperament. It does not sell predictions of luck, money or events.

**What changed in this build**
1. **Home now leads with the test, conversation and report path.** The first and largest card on Home is the path ("Take a test, talk it through in a guided conversation, then get a personal report"). The daily card is now smaller and called "Today's rhythm". The chart section is now called "Your pattern" and sits below.
2. **Wording and onboarding.** We removed "fortune" from the app's English and Spanish interface text (card titles, notifications, settings); the only remaining use is a sentence saying Saju is not fortune-telling. We also removed the Western zodiac sign card from the birth-date screen and replaced the opening headline with "Your chart shows a pattern. Now tell us if it fits."
3. **Store listing.** We removed "horoscope", "astrology" and "zodiac" from our keywords and rewrote the description and screenshot order so the test, conversation and report come first. {confirm after the listing is updated}

**How the main experience works (in this build)**
- The journey starts with a 30-question test. There are 12 tests, on themes such as attachment, burnout, focus, sleep and sensitivity. The answers, not only a birth date, shape what follows.
- The conversation is a structured, choice-based script written for each test and available in English and Spanish. It responds to the user's test answers and choices, and free-text answers are optional (the user can tap an option on every turn).
- The personal report sets the user's chart beside what they actually answered, with practical suggestions, and can be saved as a PDF.
- The chart calculation is our own engine. Solar-term boundaries are computed in-house from the sun's position, calendar conversion uses public data from the Korea Astronomy and Space Science Institute, and the engine is regression-tested against reference profiles.

**Safeguards.** We avoid predictions about health, death, accidents or disasters, and we do not use fake countdowns or scarcity. Subscriptions and one-time reports use standard in-app purchase.

**About us.** Fatesaid is made by Studio Aron, an independent developer with a registered business. Our privacy policy and terms are linked in the app and the listing.

**Review access.** Details are in the review notes: on Home, tap "Choose a test", complete the test, then continue into the conversation and the report.

We would be grateful if you would take a second look at this build. If a call would help, we are glad to arrange one, and if a specific change would let the app meet 4.3(b), please tell us and we will make it.

Thank you,
Fatesaid (Studio Aron)
